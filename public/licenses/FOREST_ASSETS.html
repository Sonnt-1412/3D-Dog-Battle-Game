Pine trail sources

The terrain surfaces, panorama and understory models are from Poly Haven (CC0). The game uses WebP textures, reduced geometry and rendered impostors for distant foliage.

  • Rico Cilliers and Rob Tuytel: Fern 02, Grass Medium 01.
  • Kless Gyzen: Rock Moss Set 01.
  • Rob Tuytel: Tree Stump 01.
  • Charlotte Baglioni: Leafy Grass.
  • Amal Kumar: Rocky Trail.
  • Andreas Mischok: Alps Field panorama.

Tree models from Tevy under CC0 1.0: Norway Spruce, Young Spruce and Scots Pine.

SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey, distributed by Mapzen Terrain Tiles. The level uses a smoothed, resampled and scaled crop from the Bidoup area of Vietnam. Source elevation tile.

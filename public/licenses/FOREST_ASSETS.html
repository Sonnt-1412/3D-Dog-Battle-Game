River valley sources

The terrain surfaces, panorama, building textures and understory models are from Poly Haven (CC0). The game uses WebP textures, reduced geometry and rendered impostors for distant foliage.

  • Rico Cilliers and Rob Tuytel: Fern 02, Grass Medium 01.
  • Kless Gyzen: Rock Moss Set 01.
  • Rob Tuytel: Tree Stump 01.
  • Charlotte Baglioni: Leafy Grass.
  • Amal Kumar: Rocky Trail.
  • Building materials: Reed Roof 04, Wood Planks and Bamboo Wall 02.
  • Andreas Mischok: Alps Field panorama.

Models from Tevy under CC0 1.0: Norway Spruce, Young Spruce, Scots Pine, Bamboo Clump, Young Bamboo Clump, Banana Plant, Common Reed and Wooden Footbridge.

Wildlife and boat models from 3DAssets.dev under CC0 1.0: Carp, Osprey, Water Buffalo and Dugout Canoe.

The village house design takes inspiration from the Tay house at the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology.

SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey, distributed by Mapzen Terrain Tiles. The level uses a smoothed, resampled and scaled crop from the Bidoup area of Vietnam. Source elevation tile.

River valley sources

The terrain surfaces, panorama, building textures and understory models are from Poly Haven (CC0). The game uses WebP textures, reduced geometry and rendered impostors for distant foliage.

  • Rico Cilliers and Rob Tuytel: Fern 02, Grass Medium 01.
  • Kless Gyzen: Rock Moss Set 01.
  • Ground surfaces include Grass Ground and Brown Mud Rocks 01.
  • Amal Kumar: Rocky Trail.
  • Building materials: Reed Roof 04, Rough Wood and Bamboo Wall 02.
  • The sky uses an adapted Hilly Terrain 01 (Pure Sky) panorama.
  • Kuutti Siitonen: Wicker Basket 02, simplified and repacked.

Models from Tevy under CC0 1.0: Norway Spruce, Scots Pine, Bamboo Clump, Young Bamboo Clump, Banana Plant, Strangler Fig, Tree Fern, Common Reed, Meadow Grass Patch, River Stones, Wooden Footbridge, Water Lily Patch, Young Corn Row, Pottery Stall and Split Firewood Stack.

The residents use the skeletons, textures and motion clips of Tevy's Casual Walker and Dog Walker (v1 LOD1, CC0). Pawtown reshaped parts of the clothing and repainted the materials for the village.

The river carp use a recolored and animated version of Tevy's Sanke Koi under CC0. The water uses the ProcTexture Ocean Normal Map under CC0.

Wildlife and boat models from 3DAssets.dev under CC0 1.0: Osprey, Dugout Canoe.

The water buffalo is adapted from the Slate Water Buffalo by JadeRealms, which incorporates an original CC0 mesh and UV layout by Lyndon Daniels. Pawtown downsampled the textures, simplified the geometry and added a skeleton and grazing motion. JadeRealms permits use of its contributions within games. See the model license for terms on original source redistribution.

The rice shoots are adapted from Rice Paddy Field Tile by Polyfork under CC BY 4.0. Pawtown extracted the shoots, scaled them and changed the colors. The field hat uses an adapted part of the Conical Hats model by 1DesignTool.

The village house design takes inspiration from the Tay house at the Vietnam Museum of Ethnology.

SRTM data courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey, distributed by Mapzen Terrain Tiles. The playable terrain near the starting village uses a smoothed, resampled and scaled crop from Bidoup, Vietnam. The distant horizon uses three rescaled profiles derived from terrain near Mù Cang Chải, Vietnam. Bidoup elevation tile.
